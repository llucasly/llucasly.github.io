One of my recent favourite pastimes is to read some of the 500 curated Wikipedia articles available [here](https://endwalker.com/archive.html).  

It's a guilty pleasure of mine, a form of "productive" procrasination. 

I encourage you to find an article to read the next time you're bored as an alternative to endless scrolling. Just search for a random number generator online, set it's min max to 1-500 and open the corresponding Wikpedia article.  

I gurantee you'll learn something new (not necessarily useful), and pick up plenty of fun little facts along the way.
